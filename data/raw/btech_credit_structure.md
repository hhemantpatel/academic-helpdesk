## Summary
- A regular B.Tech. degree requires 10 Open Elective (OE) credits out of 150 graded credits (165 including non-graded).
- A B.Tech. with Specialization/Minor requires 0 Open Elective credits; instead 8 compulsory and 12 elective specialization credits (160 graded credits).
- The total number of graded credits required for a regular B.Tech. is 150; Institute Core is 34 + 16 + 12 = 62 credits.
