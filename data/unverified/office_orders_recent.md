## Office Order 1: Academic Registration and Fee Payment Deadlines
Date: 25 July 2026
All undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral students must complete online ERP semester registration and fee payment on or before 1 August 2026. Late registration with a fine of Rs. 2,000 is permitted until 8 August 2026. No registration is entertained beyond 8 August 2026 under any circumstances.

## Office Order 2: Add / Drop and Course Audit Period
Date: 04 August 2026
The course add and drop window for Semester I, Academic Year 2026-27 is open from 1 August 2026 to 14 August 2026 (5:00 PM IST).
- Students may add elective courses subject to seat capacity and slot compatibility.
- Dropped courses within this window will not appear on the student grade sheet or academic transcript.
- Converting a credit course to an 'Audit' course requires instructor approval and must be completed by 20 August 2026.

## Office Order 3: Mandatory Procedure for Medical Leave and Excused Absence
Date: 12 August 2026
Any student seeking absence from scheduled lectures, tutorials, or laboratory sessions due to medical indisposition must:
1. Immediately report to or consult the Institute Primary Health Centre (PHC).
2. For leave extending more than 2 consecutive days, email the respective course instructor(s) and faculty advisor with the PHC medical certificate attached.
3. Inform the hostel warden if bed rest is advised.
4. Emails requesting leave must specify the Course Code, Course Title, Roll Number, Exact Absence Dates, and Medical Justification.

## Office Order 4: Examination Policy Amendment and Evaluation Norms
Date: 18 August 2026
Pursuant to Senate resolution:
1. All courses must comprise at least two continuous evaluations (quizzes/assignments), one Minor examination, and one comprehensive Major examination.
2. The weightage of the Major examination shall not exceed 50% and shall not be less than 30%.
3. Graded answer books of Minor and continuous assessments must be shown to students within 10 working days of the test date.

## Office Order 5: Institute Gazetted Holidays and Non-Instructional Days
Date: 20 August 2026
