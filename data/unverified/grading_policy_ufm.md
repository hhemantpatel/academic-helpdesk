# IIT Jodhpur Grading Policy, CGPA Calculation, and Academic Integrity / UFM
Document ID: [GRADING_UFM_POLICY]
Issuer: Senate & Office of Academic Affairs, IIT Jodhpur

## Grading Scale and Performance Levels
IIT Jodhpur follows a 10-point letter grading system to evaluate course performance:
- A+ (Outstanding / Exceptional Performance): 10 grade points.
- A (Excellent): 10 grade points.
- A- (Very Good): 9 grade points.
- B (Good): 8 grade points.
- B- (Fair): 7 grade points.
- C (Average): 6 grade points.
- C- (Below Average): 5 grade points.
- D (Marginal / Pass): 4 grade points.
- E (Poor / Incomplete): 2 grade points (may be converted via re-examination or makeup if eligible).
- F (Fail): 0 grade points (must repeat course or take replacement).
- I (Incomplete): Temporary grade due to approved medical leave or unforeseen emergency during Major Examination.
- S (Satisfactory): For non-graded mandatory courses (e.g., NSS, NSO, Community Service).
- X (Unsatisfactory): For non-graded courses.
- W (Withdrawn): Course officially withdrawn before the designated deadline.

## CGPA and SGPA Calculation
1. Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA):
   SGPA = Sum(Course Credits * Grade Points) / Sum(Course Credits in the Semester)
2. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):
   CGPA = Sum(Course Credits across all semesters * Grade Points) / Total Earned Credits across all semesters.
3. Graduation Requirements:
   - For B.Tech graduation, a student must secure a minimum CGPA of 5.0 and earn all stipulated category-wise credits (minimum 150 credits).
   - For M.Tech/M.Sc graduation, a student must secure a minimum CGPA of 6.0 (or 6.5 as amended for certain fellowships).
   - For Ph.D. coursework completion, a minimum CGPA of 7.0 is required.

## Attendance Requirement for Major Examinations
- Minimum Attendance: A student must maintain at least 75% attendance in every registered course to be eligible to sit for the Major Examination.
- In exceptional medical cases endorsed by the Institute Health Centre, condonation up to 65% may be granted by the Competent Authority.
- If attendance falls below 65%, the student is awarded a grade of 'F' on attendance grounds and is debarred from the Major Examination.

## Makeup Examination Rules
- A student who misses a Minor or Major examination solely on grounds of serious illness (with admission/treatment in Institute dispensary or recognized hospital) or approved official institutional duty may apply for a Makeup Examination.
- The student or their guardian must inform the Course Instructor and Office of Academic Affairs within 48 hours of the missed examination.
- A formal makeup request with doctor certificates must be submitted within 7 days of returning to campus.
- An 'I' grade is awarded until the makeup exam is conducted.

## Academic Integrity and Unfair Means (UFM) Policy
1. Academic dishonesty includes plagiarism, cheating in quizzes or exams, copying code or assignments, unauthorized collaboration, and impersonation.
2. Levels of Penalties:
   - Level 1 (Minor infraction in homework/lab assignment): Zero marks for the assignment and formal warning logged by instructor.
   - Level 2 (Cheating in Minor Exam / Mid-term quiz / Plagiarized term paper): Grade 'F' in the course and matter referred to the Senate Discipline Committee (SDC).
   - Level 3 (Cheating in Major Examination / Impersonation / Repeated offense): Immediate debarment from the semester, loss of academic credits, suspension for 1 to 2 semesters, or expulsion from the Institute.
