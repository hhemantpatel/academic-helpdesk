# Removed from the corpus

These files are NOT indexed and must not be cited.

- `grading_policy_ufm.md` and `office_orders_recent.md` were generated by an earlier version of
  `ingest/extract_tables.py`, not downloaded from IIT Jodhpur. They contradict the real documents.
  For example, they say 75% attendance is needed to sit the Major exam, with condonation down to 65%.
  The Senate circular IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/51 (ATTEND_POL_2024) says exam performance is no
  longer linked to attendance. They also give a Major exam weightage of 30-50%, while
  EXAM_POL_2024 says at least 40%.
- `mtech_phd_continuation_2020.pdf` is a byte-identical duplicate of
  `data/raw/continuation_norms_amendment_2020.pdf` (CONTINUATION_NORMS_2020).
